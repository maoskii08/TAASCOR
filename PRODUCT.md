# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TAASCOR HRIS serves Payroll Officers, HR teams, application administrators, executives, and authorized client operations users. They work across multiple clients, payroll periods, sites, and inconsistent source formats. Their primary job is to turn governed employee and DTR data into accurate, reviewable, auditable payroll without losing context when an exception needs correction.

## Product Purpose

TAASCOR HRIS provides employee-master management, multi-client DTR intake, identity resolution, payroll validation, reconciliation, approval, payslip generation, and operational reporting. Success means a user can upload a client file, understand every control decision, correct issues at their source, resume the same workflow state, and release only complete and evidenced payroll results.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, accountable. The product should feel capable and trustworthy under deadline pressure, explain complex payroll controls in plain language, and make the next safe action obvious.

## Anti-references

- Vague red alerts that state only that an action failed.
- Dead-end errors that force users to search through unrelated menus.
- Manual re-entry or repeated uploads after a recoverable validation issue.
- Interfaces that hide critical payroll blockers behind technical codes or generic disabled controls.
- Cluttered administration screens that mix configuration, processing, and exception resolution without hierarchy.
- Decorative enterprise-dashboard patterns that reduce readability or distract from payroll decisions.

## Design Principles

1. Explain the blocker, its operational impact, and the exact next action.
2. Route corrections to the system of record and preserve the user's client, period, batch, and filter context.
3. Keep payroll fail-closed while making recovery fast and understandable.
4. Separate configuration, processing, review, approval, and release responsibilities clearly.
5. Make evidence, ownership, and audit status visible at the point of decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, keyboard operation, focus visibility, semantic status announcements, and non-color error cues. Guidance must use plain language, support reduced motion, avoid relying on icons alone, and remain usable by payroll users working quickly on laptop-sized screens.
