## Executive Decision View

Keep the recruitment platform in foundation mode. The local engineering foundation is materially stronger and the two targeted operational control families now have executable evidence, but real external systems and organizational approvals remain unqualified. The immediate next action is an approved non-production qualification package for the mail provider, malware scanner, private-document recovery, and HRIS employee adapter.

## Final Recommendation

Retain `foundation` mode and do not deploy or activate candidate data collection. Review the local changes, then separately approve the exact commit/push scope if they should become the GitHub baseline. After that, provision an isolated non-production environment for the remaining external-system and browser/UAT gates.

## Recurring / Systemic Risks

The same activation dependencies were already open in the preceding qualification pass: real provider/scanner proof, private-document recovery, real HRIS adapter reconciliation, browser accessibility, and organizational approval. This is a systemic environment-and-ownership gap rather than a code-only gap. Assign accountable owners and dates before proposing a pilot; retain the source lock until each closure artifact is reviewed.
